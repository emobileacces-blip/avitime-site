Terms of use for testers

1. Purpose

These terms of use govern participation in the Avitime testing programme, operated by SPARKMOTION AGENCY LTD (“Avitime”). Registration and participation are free.

2. Registration

  • The programme is reserved for adult individuals, acting in a personal capacity and living in France.
  • A person may hold several accounts. However, two accounts belonging to the same person can never be selected to test the same product: a person gives at most one review per product. Other members of the same household may create their own account; accounts in the same household (same delivery address) share the product limit of the household’s highest grade.
  • The number of tester places may be limited per round. When all places are taken, sign-up is replaced by a waiting list: Avitime emails the people on it, in order of sign-up, when new places open.
  • The Tester provides accurate information and confirms their email address. Their postal address is only requested at the time of a mission.

3. Admission

Before any application, the Tester completes a profile questionnaire, reads the tester rules and takes a 15-question comprehension test. They certify that they answer alone, without help from artificial intelligence or another person.

  • 13 correct answers or more: eligible. The Tester then accepts these terms.
  • 10 to 12 correct answers: to be completed. A single new attempt is allowed.
  • Fewer than 10, or a second failure: registration not validated. A new test is possible after thirty (30) days.

Cheating on the test leads to the registration being refused.

4. Applications and selection

The Tester applies to campaigns in the catalogue, within a daily number of applications set by their grade. Applying does not guarantee selection. The final selection is made by Avitime, never by the store, according to eligibility, availability and anti-duplicate criteria, then by random draw between comparable profiles. A Tester who agrees to it may be notified by e-mail of new campaigns matching their interests (two e-mails a week at most, can be turned off at any time); these alerts invite them to apply and give no priority in the selection.

5. Mission and €0 order

  • The selected Tester confirms the mission and their delivery address within 48 hours.
  • They receive a personal order link, tied to their account, valid only for the planned product, usable only once and for a limited time. They may not pass it on or use it for other items.
  • The product and delivery are free. Avitime never asks a Tester for payment. Any request for payment must be refused and reported.

6. Testing and deadlines

  • Each mission states a recommended usage period, adapted to the product: from a few minutes to about two weeks. The Tester genuinely uses the product for the time needed to judge it, in real situations.
  • The Tester has 21 days from receipt of the product to publish their review.
  • For a digital product (online course, e-book, software, subscription), the store gives access as soon as the order is placed. The review can only be published after the usage period set by the campaign, then within the following 21 days. If access does not work, the Tester reports it from their mission.
  • An automatic reminder is sent every 7 days, by email and in their mission.
  • If prevented, they request a reasoned extension before the deadline.
  • In the event of a delivery problem (lost parcel, delay, damaged product), they report it from their mission: the deadline is suspended during the review.

7. Review content

  • The review describes the Tester’s real, personal experience. All ratings from 1 to 5 are accepted.
  • It is explicit and reasoned: it explains why the product suits or not. It is objective and judges the product as it is.
  • It shows the product in use (where, how, for how long) and includes at least one photo, which is required. The Tester may add an optional video (see article 12a).
  • It is about the product: how it is used, its finish, its packaging (box, presentation, original protection) and its quality. It does not cover delivery or shipping (delay, carrier, condition of the parcel on arrival), which do not depend on the product: a delivery problem is reported from the mission with the “Report a problem” button, and suspends the review deadline. A review about delivery or shipping is refused; the Tester may submit a new one without that part.
  • It contains no copied text, no personal data (phone, address) and no unlawful content.
  • The Tester accepts nothing in return from a store in exchange for a rating and reports any such offer, as well as any request to change their review or any contact from the store unrelated to delivery, using the “Report a problem with the store” button of their mission. They also state, in the review form, whether the store contacted them about their review.

8. Artificial intelligence

The Tester writes their review themselves. They may use a tool, including Avitime’s writing aid, to correct spelling or rephrase their own sentences, and always approve the final text. Having the review written by artificial intelligence is prohibited. A review written by artificial intelligence is refused and may lead to exclusion. Avitime carries out checks (product-specific questions, consistency with the order and delivery, detection of copied reviews and abnormal behaviour) and may ask for clarification.

9. The product and tax

The product received belongs to the Tester, whatever their review. No return or payment may be requested, including after a negative review. Defects or damage noticed on receipt are reported from the mission.

Tax. The product is provided in return for the test and the review. Its value may constitute taxable income for the Tester, who alone is responsible for declaring it. As a platform operator, Avitime collects the Tester’s tax identity (name, address, date of birth, tax number) before the first order, reports the value of products received to the tax authorities each year, and sends the Tester, before 31 January, a summary of the past year, also available in “My received products”. The value used is the product’s public price including VAT on the day of the order.

10. Breaches and sanctions

  • Missed deadline: after the 21-day deadline (including extensions) without a published review or granted extension, the tester account is excluded from the programme and no longer receives products.
  • Fraud (invented, copied or AI-written review, multiple accounts, resale of links, false evidence): the review is refused, then suspension or exclusion depending on severity.
  • Every decision is notified with reasons. The Tester may submit observations and lodge an appeal, reviewed by a person. An exclusion is never based solely on the rating given, on a negative review or on a mere report.

11. Grades, XP and reliability

Grades (Bronze, Silver, Gold, Black) determine how many products a Tester can receive: 5 per month in Bronze, 15 per month in Silver, 25 per month in Gold and 2 per day in Black. Testers move up a grade by earning points: +10 points per review published on time, +3 points for a video attached to the review, or +5 points if the video is posted on a social network under the conditions of article 12a (one video counted per review, after Avitime’s check). XP rewards participation. The reliability score measures compliance with deadlines and rules; it never depends on the rating given or on whether a review or video is positive. Thresholds are indicative and may change. A new Tester keeps a fair chance of being selected.

12. Rights in reviews and media

The Tester remains the author of their review. By publishing it, they authorise Avitime and the store concerned to display it, with its photos, on the tested product’s page and on the Avitime website, under a pseudonym or their first name, for as long as the product is marketed. They warrant that they are the author of the published media and that these do not show identifiable third parties without their consent.

12a. Tester videos

The Tester may, if they wish, attach to their review a short video of the product in use (60 seconds and 50 MB maximum) and/or the link to a video they have posted on a social network (TikTok, Instagram, YouTube). This video is optional: it is never a condition for receiving a product, and its content, positive or negative, has no effect on the Tester’s selection, points or grade.

  • Required label. As the product was given free of charge, a video posted on a social network is a commercial communication. The Tester labels it clearly, legibly and identifiably with “Ad” or “Paid partnership” (in French: « Publicité » or « Collaboration commerciale »), or with the social network’s branded-content tool, in accordance with French Law No. 2023-451 of 9 June 2023. A video that does not comply is refused.
  • Check. Avitime checks each video before displaying it and counting the points: valid link, product visible, label present, no identifiable third parties without their consent and no unlawful content. The opinion expressed is never a reason for refusal.
  • Permissions. By sending the video, the Tester allows Avitime and the store concerned to view it in their accounts. Using separate boxes, they may also allow: (a) its display under their review, on the tested product’s page and on the Avitime website, for as long as the product is marketed; (b) its use in ads by the store on social networks, using the tools those networks provide (for example an ad code the Tester sends through Avitime), for 12 months; (c) its reposting by Avitime on its own social accounts, crediting the Tester. These permissions are granted free of charge. The Tester may withdraw permissions (a) and (c) at any time from their mission, and permission (b) for the future, without affecting ads already running.
  • No direct contact. The store does not contact the Tester about their video: any request (for example an ad code) goes through Avitime.
  • Warranties. The Tester warrants that they are the author of the video, hold the rights to the music and other elements used, and that the video does not show identifiable third parties without their consent.

13. General provisions

Personal data. See the privacy policy.

Changes. In the event of a material change to these terms or the rules, new acceptance is requested before the next mission.

Closing the account. The Tester may close their account at any time, after their current missions have ended.

Governing law and disputes. In case of difficulty, please first write to contact@avitime.fr: we will seek an amicable solution. Failing that, the dispute is brought before the competent court under the applicable legal rules, without prejudice to your rights as a consumer.
