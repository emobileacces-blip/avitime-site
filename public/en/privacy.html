1. Data controller

SPARKMOTION AGENCY LTD, a company registered in England and Wales (no. 16880032), Dept 6341, 43 Owston Road, Carcroft, Doncaster, DN6 8DA, United Kingdom, which operates the Avitime brand.

Contact: contact@avitime.fr

2. Data collected

  • Testers: first name, last name, email address, phone number where needed, age range, interests, answers to the questionnaire and admission test, delivery address (at the time of a mission), date of birth and tax identification number (before the first order), value of products received, mission history, reviews, photos and videos, reports and messages.
  • Merchants: identity of the business and its representatives, business contact details, billing data, store data needed for the service (products, test orders, shipping statuses).
  • All users: login and browsing data (IP address, technical logs), fraud-prevention signals.

Avitime does not read or store the personal data of a store’s other customers.

4. Recipients

  • Authorised Avitime staff.
  • The store concerned by a mission, which receives the tester’s name, delivery address and phone number and an Avitime relay e-mail address (never their real e-mail address), only to fulfil and ship the order. Avitime’s terms forbid it to use them for marketing or to contact the tester about their review.
  • Avitime’s technical service providers (hosting and email: Hostinger; payment: Stripe for PrestaShop and WooCommerce stores, Shopify Billing for Shopify stores), bound by confidentiality.
  • The tax authorities, for the annual report of the value of products received by testers.
  • Public authorities, where required by law.

Published reviews are visible to the public, under a pseudonym or first name.

5. Transfers

Avitime is operated from the United Kingdom, which benefits from an adequacy decision of the European Commission. Any other transfer is made with the safeguards provided by applicable law.

7. Automated decisions

The admission test is marked automatically, and tester selection combines automatic criteria with a random draw. Anyone may ask for human intervention, express their point of view and contest a decision concerning them, in particular a refused admission or an exclusion.

8. Your rights

You have the right to access, rectify, erase, restrict, object to and port your data, to withdraw your consent at any time and to set instructions on what happens to your data after your death. To exercise them: contact@avitime.fr. You may also lodge a complaint with the French data protection authority (CNIL, www.cnil.fr) or the UK Information Commissioner’s Office (ico.org.uk).

9. Security

Avitime applies appropriate measures: encryption in transit (HTTPS), encryption at rest of sensitive data (addresses, phone numbers, tax numbers, tracking numbers, store access keys), access control, single-use order links, logging of sensitive actions and backups. Card data is processed exclusively by the payment provider.

10. Cookies

Cookies required for the site to work (login, security, language) are set without consent. Audience measurement cookies are only set with your agreement, which you can withdraw at any time.

11. Minors

The testing programme is reserved for adults. The data of a minor who tries to sign up is deleted.

Real customer reviews, written by real testers. Every review is labelled “Free product for testing”.
