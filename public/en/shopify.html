How the app works

  1. You pick a product from your Shopify catalog and the number of testers.
  2. Avitime selects the testers among those who applied: adults living in your store’s country, chosen by Avitime and never by the store.
  3. Each tester claims a $0 order from their Avitime account. It’s a normal Shopify order, tagged “avitime”, that you ship with your usual carriers.
  4. The tester uses the product and posts a review within 21 days of delivery: rating, pros, cons and photos.
  5. Avitime checks the review (order, delivery, content) and shows it on your product page.

Install Avitime on Shopify in 3 steps

  1. Open the Avitime listing on the Shopify App Store and click “Install”.
  2. Approve the app: it opens in your Shopify admin and links your store to your Avitime account.
  3. Add the “Avitime reviews” block (and the stars block under the title if you like) to your product page from the theme editor: Online Store → Themes → Customize.

The app only asks for what it needs: read your products, create the tester orders and track their shipping. It never changes your prices or your customers’ orders.

What your visitors see

  • A review block on the product page, with the testers’ photos and your public reply.
  • Star rating and average score, with structured data (Product, rating, reviews) that Google can read. Google decides whether to show the stars in its results.
  • The “Tester review · free product” label on every review. It’s required: under the FTC Endorsement Guides (16 CFR Part 255), a review given in exchange for a free product must say so.

Already using Judge.me, Loox or another reviews app?

Keep it. A reviews app collects reviews from your customers after they buy; Avitime gets you reviews before the sales come in, on the products you choose. Both blocks can sit on the same product page: your customers’ reviews on one side, tester reviews, clearly labeled, on the other.

Frequently asked questions

Is the Avitime app free?

Installing the app and your first 5 tests are free, with no credit card. You only pay for the products sent to testers and their shipping. After that, plans start at $33.90 per month, billed by Shopify.

Who chooses the testers?

Avitime, never the store. Testers are adults who apply to the products they’re interested in and live in your store’s country: a US store’s products go to testers in the US.

Can I delete a negative review?

No. Every review is published whatever the rating: that’s what makes the other reviews credible. You can reply publicly, then improve the product page or the product. A review that only talks about shipping is rejected.

Is this legal under FTC rules?

Yes, when it’s disclosed. The FTC allows reviews from people who received a free product as long as the connection is clearly disclosed and the review isn’t conditioned on a positive opinion. That’s why every review carries the “Tester review · free product” label and is published whatever the rating.

Do tester orders affect my sales figures?

They’re $0 Shopify orders tagged “avitime”: they don’t inflate your revenue and you can filter them in one click.

Can I uninstall the app?

Yes, anytime from Shopify. Finish your running campaigns first so testers can post their reviews. The subscription stops when you uninstall.
