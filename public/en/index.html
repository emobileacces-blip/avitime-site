Compliance & the law

Buying reviews or posting
fake reviews: what does the law say?

In France, publishing or commissioning fake reviews (guide in French) is a misleading commercial practice, punishable by heavy penalties. Having a product tested, however, is legal, provided you disclose it clearly.

  • €300,000maximum fine

  • 2 yearsmaximum prison sentence

  • 10%of average annual turnover (possible cap on the fine)

What is forbidden

  • Publishing fake reviews, or having them published.
  • Posing as a consumer.
  • Presenting reviews as verified without checking them.
  • Hiding that a review was obtained in exchange for a benefit.

What Avitime does

  • Testers selected by Avitime, never by the shop.
  • Every rating from 1 to 5 is published, as long as the review is authentic.

FAQ

Questions about customer reviews
and product tests

Here are the answers to the most common questions about how Avitime works, product tests, reviews and the terms of use. Click a question to see the answer.

What if a tester gives a low rating?

The review is published. The shop can neither edit nor delete it: that is what makes the other reviews credible. You can reply publicly, then improve the product page or the product. A review that only talks about delivery is rejected, and the tester reports the problem separately.

How much does it really cost?

Your first 5 tests are free, with no subscription and no credit card. Products and shipping are at your expense: with a €12 product and €6 shipping, 5 testers cost €90. After that, the subscription starts at €39.90 excl. VAT per month billed annually, or €49.90 excl. VAT per month with no commitment.

How many reviews will a shop get?

Avitime does not guarantee any number of reviews. Each free product is actually ordered and delivered to a selected tester, who commits to publishing a review within the agreed time. The dashboard shows exactly how many missions are completed.

Can a shop choose its testers?

No. The shop sets the campaign criteria (country, category, exclusions), but the final selection is made by Avitime. This is what keeps the reviews independent.

Who ships the products?

The shop, with its usual carriers. Avitime neither stores nor ships products. On a site without a plugin, the selected tester’s address appears in your merchant space, and you enter the tracking number.

Which products can be tested?

Your shop must deliver to France, and the product tested must be the one your customers receive. Sensitive categories (health, supplements, nicotine, alcohol, products for minors…) follow a strict eligibility policy. Digital products are accepted.
