What the law says

Fake reviews are illegal. Real reviews are earned.

Across the EU (Omnibus Directive) and in the UK (DMCC Act 2024), fake reviews are banned. In France, publishing or commissioning fake reviews, posing as a customer, or presenting reviews as verified without verifying them are misleading commercial practices, enforced by the DGCCRF. French penalties:

€300,000maximum fine
2 yearsmaximum prison sentence
10%of average annual turnover: what the fine can reach

What is forbidden

  • Publishing fake reviews, or having someone else publish them.
  • Posing as a consumer.
  • Editing customer reviews to show a product in a better light.
  • Presenting reviews as verified without a verification process.

How Avitime works, in six steps

1 / 6 · The starting point

A good product. No reviews.

Without reviews, even an excellent product stays invisible. Avitime hands it to real consumers so they can talk about it.

2 / 6 · The selection

Avitime picks the testers. Never the store.

Age, country, category, duplicate checks, availability. Then a random draw between comparable profiles, for a fair and explainable selection.

3 / 6 · The order

A real order. At €0.

The tester receives a secure link, tied to their account, the product and the mission. The store’s Avitime module checks everything server-side. One authorisation, one single order.

4 / 6 · The delivery

The store ships as usual.

Avitime follows the order status. The testing period starts on delivery and adapts to the product category.

5 / 6 · The test

They test it. Then they say what they think.

A 1 to 5 rating, pros, limits, real usage time. Positive or negative, the review is published, and the tester keeps the product.

6 / 6 · Publication

A real review, on the product page.

With the label “Free product provided for testing — verified Avitime tester”. That transparency is what makes the review credible.

Two spaces

Stores on one side. Real consumers on the other.

Each has its own space. Avitime keeps control of what matters: selection, ordering, publication.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions about Avitime

Are fake reviews illegal?

Yes. In France, publishing or commissioning fake reviews, posing as a consumer or presenting reviews as verified without verifying them are misleading commercial practices. According to the DGCCRF (the French consumer protection authority), they are punishable by two years’ imprisonment and a €300,000 fine, which can be raised to 10% of average annual turnover. Fake reviews are also banned across the EU (Omnibus Directive) and in the UK (DMCC Act 2024).

How many reviews will a store get?

Avitime guarantees no volume of reviews. Every free product is really ordered and delivered to a selected tester, who commits to publishing a review within the agreed time. The dashboard shows exactly how many missions are completed.

Can a store choose its testers?

No. It sets the campaign criteria (country, category, exclusions), but the final selection is made by Avitime. That is what keeps reviews independent.

What happens if a review is negative?

It is published, just like a positive one, if it is genuine and meets the moderation rules. The tester keeps the product and is never penalised for their rating.

Who ships the products?

The store, with its usual carriers. Avitime neither stores nor ships products.

Which platforms are supported?

PrestaShop and WooCommerce, with a dedicated module and extension you download from your merchant account. Shopify: the Avitime app is being reviewed for the Shopify App Store. More platforms will follow.

I am a tester: how much does it cost?

Nothing. Sign-up is free, the product and shipping are free, and there is no subscription.

Which products can be tested?

The pilot starts with non-regulated accessories. Sensitive categories (health, supplements, nicotine, alcohol, products for minors…) follow a strict eligibility policy.

Is dropshipping accepted?

Yes. The tester orders from your store like any customer, your supplier ships, and the review deadline starts at delivery. The product tested must be exactly the one your customers receive.

Can I have an online course or a digital product tested?

Soon. The digital product flow (online courses, e-books, software) is ready on the platform and being tested with the modules: the tester gets access as soon as they order, and their review only opens after a real period of use set by the campaign. Write to us at contact@avitime.fr to be among the first.

Can a store contact a tester about their review?

No. The store receives the tester’s name, address and phone number only for delivery, plus a relay e-mail address: never their real e-mail. Asking them for a good rating, offering them anything in return or marketing to them is forbidden by our terms. The tester can report it in one click from their mission, and the store risks suspension.
